# Data-subject rights runbook

Intake address: `info@ascentia-gp.com`  
Default deadline: one month from receipt, after logging the original receipt
time. Any lawful extension must be communicated with reasons within that first
month.

## Workflow

1. Record the request time, channel, requested right, account email, assigned
   owner, deadline, and every action in the restricted rights-request log.
2. Acknowledge receipt promptly. Ask only for the minimum evidence needed to
   confirm identity; do not routinely request a government ID when account
   authentication or a proportionate alternative is sufficient.
3. Clarify scope if necessary without resetting the original deadline. Check
   whether the requester acts for another person and verify that authority.
4. Search the account/profile, jobs and live media, exports, usage ledger,
   feedback inbox, consumer-contract vault, purchase/withdrawal/refund records,
   erasure journal, and applicable processor records. Search Stripe/Google/
   ElevenLabs only through authorised account tools and only to the necessary
   extent.
5. Apply the requested right. For erasure, remove live account/job/media data
   and trigger the replayable erasure path. Do not erase tax, accounting,
   payment, unresolved withdrawal/refund, fraud, or legal-claim evidence while
   a documented lawful retention obligation applies; restrict it where required.
6. Have a second authorised person review the response, exceptions, recipients,
   and attachments. Use a secure delivery channel and do not expose data of
   other people.
7. Tell the requester what was done, what was not done and why, the right to
   complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, and any available
   judicial remedy. Record completion time and evidence.

## Right-specific checks

- **Access/export:** the in-product export is a convenience, not the limit of
  Article 15. Include purposes, categories, recipients, retention, source,
  rights, and transfer safeguards where applicable.
- **Rectification:** update incorrect account data and notify processors or
  recipients where required and feasible.
- **Erasure:** run account/job deletion and processor deletion; preserve only
  a documented exception and the pseudonymous 30-day erasure proof.
- **Restriction:** block ordinary use while preserving the restricted record
  and any permitted legal-claim processing.
- **Portability:** provide user-supplied/observed data in the existing structured
  export where Article 20 applies; derived security assessments need not be
  treated as portable merely because they exist.
- **Objection:** stop legitimate-interest processing unless compelling grounds
  or legal claims justify continuation. Direct marketing is not used and would
  require an unconditional stop.
- **Consent withdrawal:** stop future consent-based processing without treating
  prior lawful processing as invalid.
- **Automated decisions:** GSUBS currently states that it makes no solely
  automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect. Escalate any
  product change that contradicts this statement before release.

Requests are normally free. A refusal or fee for a manifestly unfounded or
excessive request requires documented evidence and controller approval; never
make that decision automatically.
