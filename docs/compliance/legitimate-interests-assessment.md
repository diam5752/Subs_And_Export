# Legitimate interests assessment

Review date: 2026-08-28

## Purposes

Ascentia relies on legitimate interests only for proportionate security,
abuse/fraud prevention, service integrity, support, product feedback, and the
establishment or defence of legal claims. Core account, media-processing, and
paid-service delivery instead rely primarily on contract; tax/accounting and
rights handling rely on legal obligations where applicable.

## Necessity

The selected records are limited to what the current controls need: rate-limit
and security signals, pseudonymous feedback abuse keys, bounded page paths,
job/credit integrity events, and support correspondence. Raw feedback network
addresses are not stored in the feedback record. Advertising, cross-service
tracking, sale of data, and Ascentia-model training are outside the purpose.
A less intrusive aggregate or pseudonymous signal should replace an identified
record whenever it can achieve the same control.

## Balancing

Users reasonably expect an online account and paid processing service to
protect accounts, prevent duplicate spend/abuse, diagnose failures, and answer
support messages. Possible impacts include unwanted linkage, mistaken abuse
classification, or longer-than-expected support records. Those risks are
reduced through short purpose-specific retention, authentication, restricted
access, pseudonymisation, no advertising profile, user export/deletion tools,
and the right to object. No legitimate-interest signal currently produces a
solely automated legal or similarly significant decision.

## Outcome and stop conditions

The current limited processing is provisionally necessary and balanced. This
assessment does not cover optional analytics, behavioural advertising,
biometrics, model training, child profiling, data brokerage, or combining GSUBS
data with unrelated products. Introducing any of those, materially extending
retention, or using a security score to deny service without human review stops
reliance on this assessment and requires a new lawful-basis/DPIA review before
release.
