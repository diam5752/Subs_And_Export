# GSUBS UI polish audit — 2026-07-30

## Scope and design reference

The audit covered the public landing page, authentication, legal/offline pages,
upload states, cost confirmation, the completed subtitle editor, the player,
style and transcript controls, export, account settings, history, slow startup,
keyboard use, and responsive layouts from 320 px mobile through 1440 px desktop.

FlowCV was used only as a product-design reference: keep the current task
prominent, make the preview react immediately, keep controls compact and close
to the preview, and keep export easy to find. GSUBS retains its own visual
identity and video-first interaction model.

Selected browser evidence is committed explicitly under the normally ignored
artifact directory `output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/`. Every accepted change
below has a before/after capture there.

## Findings and visual re-critique

### F001 — Completed mobile editor began below a redundant marketing hero

- Before: [320 px transcript](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/editor-transcript-mobile-320x568.png)
- Problem: the marketing headline and stepper consumed the first viewport, so
  the player and settings were not available as one editing task.
- Fix: the completed state now enters a compact workspace mode; the marketing
  intro is removed, the stepper is hidden only at compact mobile widths, and
  the phone scales down on short screens.
- After: [final 320 px editor](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/editor-transcript-mobile-final-320x568.png),
  [430 px editor](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/editor-transcript-mobile-viewport-430x932.png),
  [desktop editor](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/editor-transcript-desktop-viewport-1440x900.png)
- Re-critique: player, New Video, Export and editor tabs now fit in the first
  320 px viewport without horizontal overflow. Desktop retains the useful
  workflow breadcrumb.

### F002 — Google login showed the same configuration failure twice

- Before: [320 px login](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/login-mobile-320x568.png)
- Problem: the localized unavailable notice was followed by the raw English
  initialization error.
- Fix: initialization failure has one localized status; credential-exchange
  failures remain independently visible.
- After: [320 px login](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/login-mobile-viewport-320x568.png)
- Re-critique: one calm status remains, with no contradictory or duplicated
  copy.

### F003 — Cookie consent dominated the mobile viewport and looked translucent

- Before: [430 px landing](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/landing-mobile-430x932.png)
- Problem: the sheet was visually heavy and underlying content reduced text
  clarity.
- Fix: opaque white surface, compact typography and spacing, hidden decorative
  icon on mobile, semantic description, and 44 px actions.
- After: [430 px consent](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/landing-cookie-mobile-final-430x932.png)
- Re-critique: the sheet is readable, under 180 px high, and no longer competes
  with the upload task.

### F004 — Footer fragmented at narrow widths

- Before: [320 px landing](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/landing-mobile-320x568.png)
- Problem: brand and legal links were cramped into one row.
- Fix: below 480 px, brand and links form two centered rows with bounded gaps.
- After: [320 px full page](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/landing-mobile-full-320x568.png),
  [430 px full page](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/landing-mobile-full-430x932.png)
- Re-critique: the footer reads as one deliberate signature and both legal
  links remain inside the viewport.

### F005 — Mobile login and registration prioritized marketing over the form

- Before: [320 px login](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/login-mobile-320x568.png),
  [320 px registration](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/register-mobile-320x568.png)
- Problem: on short phones the primary account task started too low.
- Fix: the marketing promise is removed at compact mobile widths and auth
  spacing is reduced; desktop keeps the two-column story.
- After: [320 px login](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/login-mobile-full-320x568.png),
  [320 px registration](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/register-mobile-full-320x568.png)
- Re-critique: the form now starts immediately below the header while still
  feeling spacious.

### F006 — The transcript tab was truncated to “Απομαγνητοφ…”

- Before: [320 px transcript](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/editor-transcript-mobile-320x568.png)
- Problem: the visible label looked broken at the minimum supported width.
- Fix: the visible compact label becomes “Υπότιτλοι” at 350 px and below while
  the accessible tab name remains “Απομαγνητοφώνηση”.
- After: [final 320 px editor](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/editor-transcript-mobile-final-320x568.png)
- Re-critique: both tabs are complete, balanced and unambiguous.

### F007 — Several mobile hit areas were below 44 px

- Before: [430 px editor](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/editor-transcript-mobile-430x932.png),
  [430 px account](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/account-modal-mobile-430x932.png)
- Problem: header actions, cue controls, language controls, account actions and
  the inline subtitle trigger had small touch surfaces.
- Fix: critical controls now expose at least a 44 px touch target; the inline
  subtitle trigger receives an invisible expanded hit surface without changing
  its visual size.
- After: [final 320 px editor](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/editor-transcript-mobile-final-320x568.png),
  [430 px account](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/account-modal-mobile-final-430x932.png)
- Re-critique: controls remain visually compact but are materially easier to
  acquire by touch.

### F008 — One cue edit label mixed Greek and English

- Before: [desktop transcript](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/editor-transcript-desktop-viewport-1440x900.png)
- Problem: assistive technology received “Επεξεργασία cue at 0:00”.
- Fix: the entire label is localized with the cue time as a parameter.
- After: [desktop transcript](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/editor-transcript-desktop-viewport-1440x900.png)
- Re-critique: the visible design is unchanged while the semantic experience is
  now consistent in both languages.

### F009 — Slow authentication looked like a blank page

- Before: [430 px loading](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/loading-state-mobile-430x932.png),
  [desktop loading](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/loading-state-desktop-1440x900.png)
- Problem: a single muted “Φόρτωση…” in empty space made slow connections look
  stalled.
- Fix: branded, layout-stable loading screen with an accessible live status,
  subtle waveform animation and a reduced-motion version.
- After: [430 px loading](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/loading-state-mobile-430x932.png),
  [desktop loading](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/loading-state-desktop-1440x900.png)
- Re-critique: the wait is clearly intentional without turning into a splash
  screen or hiding the cookie decision.

### F010 — History item actions were too small for touch

- Before: [430 px history](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/history-modal-mobile-430x932.png)
- Problem: View measured about 42 px high and the delete icon about 32×24 px.
- Fix: Download and View are at least 44 px high; delete, confirm and cancel are
  44×44 px.
- After: [430 px polished history](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/history-modal-mobile-polished-430x932.png)
- Re-critique: every destructive or navigational action has a reliable touch
  surface without oversized icons.

### F011 — History actions crushed the filename on mobile

- Before: [first touch-target pass](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/history-modal-mobile-final-430x932.png)
- Problem: fixing the hit areas exposed a cramped one-row layout and reduced the
  filename to “Gr…”.
- Fix: below the small breakpoint, metadata uses the full card width and the
  three actions form a separate aligned row.
- After: [430 px polished history](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/history-modal-mobile-polished-430x932.png)
- Re-critique: filename, date and expiry are readable; the action row is still
  compact and scans left-to-right.

### F012 — Account/history dialog lacked complete keyboard behavior

- Before: [430 px account](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/before/account-modal-mobile-430x932.png)
- Problem: Escape did not close the dialog, initial focus was not deliberate,
  and focus was not restored to the avatar.
- Fix: focus begins on Close, Tab/Shift+Tab stay inside the dialog, Escape
  closes it, and focus returns to the profile trigger.
- After: [430 px account](../../output/playwright/ui-qa-2026-07-30/after/account-modal-mobile-final-430x932.png)
- Re-critique: visual layout remains unchanged while keyboard behavior now
  matches a complete modal contract.

## QA infrastructure correction

The first iOS WebKit player run exposed a timing race in the test itself. The
test played a four-second mock cue at 2× and then attempted a pinch after WebKit
had correctly advanced beyond that cue, so the overlay no longer existed. The
test now pauses and seeks back inside the mock cue before checking pinch. The
production behavior was not changed, and the complete Chromium, Android
Chromium, iOS WebKit and Firefox matrix passes.
