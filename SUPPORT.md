# Support

Choose the channel that matches the problem so it can be handled safely.

## Product bugs

Use the repository's structured bug-report form for reproducible problems that can
be demonstrated with mock or fully redacted data. Include the device, browser,
affected product surface and concise reproduction steps.

Do not attach customer videos, transcripts, email addresses, access tokens,
cookies, payment data or other personal information.

## Ideas and product feedback

Use the feature-request form for public, non-sensitive suggestions. Users of the
hosted product can also use its in-app feedback control for ideas, bugs and general
comments.

## Security, privacy and exposed credentials

Do not open a public issue. Use the
[private security advisory form](https://github.com/diam5752/Subs_And_Export/security/advisories/new)
and provide only the minimum redacted evidence needed to reproduce the problem.
See `SECURITY.md` for the supported version and security boundaries.

## Account, billing and production incidents

Do not post account identifiers, receipts, media or production logs publicly. Use
the hosted product's private feedback channel and describe the time and visible
symptom without including credentials or private content.
